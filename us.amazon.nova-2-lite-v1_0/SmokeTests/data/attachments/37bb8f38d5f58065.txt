The City of Wahkon has a formal process for handling complaints and concerns. You can submit your complaint in writing to the City Administrator or City Council, or you can bring it up during a regular City Council meeting. The council meets on the second and fourth Tuesdays of each month at 7:00 PM in the City Hall. For more information, you can visit the city website or contact City Hall directly.
